Tenancy and isolation

  • Tenant-scoped records with row-level isolation enforced in the database, not only in the UI.
  • Every authorization decision, execution and retest is written with its tenant binding.
  • Connector credentials are scoped to the minimum the connector needs; read-only first.

What we log — and what we never log

  • Log: decision, actor identity, tool and capability, outcome, timestamp, retest result.
  • Never log: raw secrets, bearer tokens, full request bodies containing credentials. Sensitive keys are redacted at the boundary.
  • Missing evidence is recorded as UNKNOWN, UNMEASURABLE or INSUFFICIENT_REACH — it is never replaced with a success state.

Retention and deletion

Retention is agreed in the engagement scope before any data is ingested. At the end of an engagement you can request deletion, and you receive written confirmation. Evidence exports are yours to keep.

Disclosure

Report vulnerabilities to security@elitze.info. We acknowledge within 48 hours, triage within 5 business days, and coordinate disclosure for 90 days. Good-faith research within a written scope is authorised; nothing outside that scope is.
